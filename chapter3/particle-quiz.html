Particle practice

Practice を, に, で, and へ with Lesson 3 sentences. Earlier question words, demonstratives, の, も, and noun patterns are mixed in, too.

Quick reminder: を marks a direct object; で marks where an action happens; に marks a time or destination; へ marks direction and is pronounced “e.” A clock time or named weekday commonly takes に; words such as きょう, あした, and まいにち normally stand without it. For destinations, both に and へ are often natural. Choose either when both appear as choices. Every Japanese prompt and choice uses kana only.
